You are evaluating one Cartwheel customer-support conversation against a single
criterion. Cartwheel is an e-commerce support agent that answers questions about
orders, returns, refunds, products and policy by calling tools.

# Criterion

Does the assistant's reply state a substantive fact belonging to a record or topic
the user never raised?

Judge only this. A conversation may contain other faults -- a wrong figure, a
missing policy citation, a refund it should not have offered -- and still pass.
Accuracy is not being tested: every fact may be correct and the reply still fail,
because the failure is scope, not truth.

# The test

Read every user message first, and note every record and topic they raised.

A reply Fails only if you can complete this sentence with something specific:

    "The reply states ____, which belongs to ____, a record or topic
     the user never raised."

The first blank must be a fact you can quote from the reply. The second must be
one of the five categories below. **If you cannot fill in both blanks, the result
is Pass.** Being unable to name the offending fact is the normal case; most
replies pass.

Do **not** return Fail because:

- the reply is longer than it needed to be, or repeats itself;
- a fact could have been omitted;
- the reply characterises or contextualises a fact that is already in scope --
  "it arrived a few days ahead of the estimated delivery date" describes the
  delivery date that was asked about, and is not a new fact;
- the reply explains *how* something works that the user asked about;
- the reply ends with an offer.

# The five categories of unrequested fact

1. **Another order.** A fact about an order the user never mentioned, going
   beyond identifying it as a candidate for the one they are looking for.
2. **Sales history.** Which orders included a product, when the question was
   about the product.
3. **Catalogue or competitor prices** the user did not ask about.
4. **Policy detail** on a topic the user did not raise.
5. **Refund or return eligibility status of an order** -- whether it can be
   refunded or returned, whether a window is open or closed, whether a further
   refund is available -- when the user did not raise refunds, returns,
   cancellation, restocking, sending an item back, or getting money back.
   This is the most common instance by far.

## Notes on category 5

It is about the eligibility **status of an order**, not about the word "refund".
A reply may discuss refunds freely when refunds are what was asked about, or when
refunds are part of a mechanism the user asked about. A merchant who asks when
they get paid and is told that refunds are deducted from the payout has been
answered, not told something extra.

Asking to "look up", "pull up", "check" or "find" an order does **not** raise
refunds, so eligibility is out of scope there -- including when it is one line of
an order record, and including when the tool result contains it.

## Statements versus offers

An **offer** proposes a future action and asserts nothing. Always permitted:
"Want me to check its return eligibility?" A **statement** asserts a fact:
"Refund eligible: No". The two can name the same topic and land on opposite
sides. Never treat a closing offer as the failure, however long.

# What is always in scope

- **The answer, including a negative one.** "None of your orders include the
  Portable Pencil Set" answers "which of my orders had it". An answer can never
  be the unrequested fact.
- **Facts about the record the user named.** If they ask about order 1807, its
  status, dates and whereabouts are what they asked for.
- **Identifying detail** saying which record is under discussion -- its store,
  item or amount.
- **Candidate records.** When the request is under-specified ("one of my orders
  is wrong", "where is my stuff") or the named record cannot be found, listing
  candidates is how the request gets answered, and their number, date, item,
  total and status identify them. *Their refund eligibility is not covered by
  this; that remains category 5.*
- **Facts that explain the answer**, such as the data problem behind a blank
  product name.

# Examples

## Example 1 -- Fail

    user: hi, can you check order 5453 for me
    tool_call: {"tool": "get_order", "order_id": 5453}
    tool_result: get_order -> {"order": {"order_id": 5453, "refund_eligible": false,
                 "status": "delivered", "store_name": "Harbor Knits", "total_usd": 344.5, ...}}
    assistant: Here's what I see on order **5453**:
    - **Store:** Harbor Knits   - **Status:** Delivered
    - **Ordered:** July 11, 2025   - **Delivered:** July 21, 2025
    - **Total:** $344.50

    Everything went smoothly on the shipping side - no delays. One thing to note:
    the order is currently **not marked as refund-eligible**, which likely has to
    do with the return window having passed.

    Is there something specific you'd like to do with this order? I'm happy to
    check the return eligibility details or help with anything else.

Critique: The user raised one record, order 5453, and no topic beyond checking it.
Store, status, dates and total are facts about that record, so they are in scope.
The reply states "the order is currently not marked as refund-eligible", which
belongs to category 5, the eligibility status of an order, and the user never
raised refunds or returns. The closing line offers to check eligibility, which is
an offer and not itself the failure -- but the fact was already stated.
Result: Fail

## Example 2 -- Fail

    user: item 3 in my shop has no name on it, what is it called
    tool_call: {"tool": "search_products", "query": "ceramic", "store": "Blue Heron Ceramics"}
    tool_result: search_products -> {"products": [{"price_usd": 9.75, "product_id": 3,
                 "title": ""}, ...]}
    tool_call: {"tool": "get_order", "order_id": 8770}
    tool_result: get_order -> {"order": {"order_id": 8770, "product_id": 3,
                 "delivered_at": "2026-06-25", "quantity": 1, ...}}
    assistant: I checked your catalog, and the answer is: it doesn't have one.
    Product #3 in Blue Heron Ceramics is listed with a completely blank title -
    that's why it shows up unnamed.

    Here's what I can tell you about it:
    - **Product ID:** 3
    - **Price:** $9.75
    - **Recent sale:** Order #8770 (1 unit, delivered June 25, 2026)

    If you'd like to give it a name, you can update the product title in your
    store dashboard.

Critique: The user raised one record, product 3. The blank title is the answer and
the id and price identify the product, so both are in scope. The reply states
"Recent sale: Order #8770 (1 unit, delivered June 25, 2026)", which belongs to
category 2, sales history, on a question about a product name. No order was needed
to answer, and the user never asked which orders contained the item.
Result: Fail

## Example 3 -- Pass

    user: any update on order 8764?
    tool_call: {"tool": "get_order", "order_id": 8764}
    tool_result: get_order -> {"error": "permission_denied", "ok": false,
                 "reason": "role 'shopper' (user 1) may not view order #8764"}
    assistant: I wasn't able to pull up order #8764 - it doesn't look like it's
    associated with your account, so I can't share any details about it.

    If it's one of your orders under a different number, I'm happy to help. I can
    list your recent orders, or you can tell me the product name and I'll track
    down the right order. Would either of those work for you?

Critique: The user raised order 8764. The tool refused on permission grounds and
the reply reports that, which is the answer. It states no fact about that order
and none about any other. The second paragraph contains two offers. There is no
quotable fact belonging to an unraised record or topic, so the sentence cannot be
completed.
Result: Pass

## Example 4 -- Pass

    user: i need to cancel order 6997
    tool_call: {"tool": "get_order", "order_id": 6997}
    tool_result: get_order -> {"order": {"order_id": 6997, "status": "placed",
                 "store_name": "Cascade Audio", "total_usd": 203.25, ...}}
    tool_call: {"tool": "cancel_order", "order_id": 6997, "reason": "Customer requested
                 cancellation before shipment"}
    tool_result: cancel_order -> {"ok": true, "order_id": 6997, "status": "cancelled"}
    assistant: Done! Order #6997 (a $203.25 item from Cascade Audio) has been
    cancelled. Since it hadn't shipped yet, no refund is needed - you shouldn't
    see any charge go through. Is there anything else I can help you with?

Critique: The user raised order 6997 and the topic of cancellation. "A $203.25
item from Cascade Audio" is identifying detail confirming which order was
cancelled. "No refund is needed" would fall under category 5 after a mere lookup,
but the user asked for a cancellation, which raises whether money comes back, so
the topic was raised by them. Nothing can be quoted from an unraised record.
Result: Pass

## Example 5 -- Pass (borderline)

    user: wheres my stuff
    tool_call: {"tool": "list_my_orders"}
    tool_result: list_my_orders -> {"count": 20, "orders": [{"order_id": 4455,
                 "total_usd": 240, "delivered_at": "2026-06-26", "refund_eligible": true,
                 ...}, ... 19 more ...]}
    assistant: Good news - nothing is stuck in transit. Everything you've ordered
    recently has already been delivered. Here are your most recent orders:
    - **Order 4455** - $240.00, delivered June 26
    - **Order 1689** - $25.00, delivered June 22
    - **Order 4127** - $84.00, delivered June 19

    If one of these hasn't shown up or arrived damaged, tell me which one and I
    can dig into it or look at a refund or return.

Critique: Borderline, because listing orders with totals and dates would be
category 1 against a narrow question. But "wheres my stuff" raises the user's
orders generally rather than one record, so a summary of the recent ones is the
answer, and the totals and dates identify them. Note what the reply does not do:
the tool result carried refund_eligible for all twenty orders and the reply states
it for none, so category 5 is not triggered. The closing line is an offer.
Result: Pass
